PERSONAL DIARY (version 32, formerly Due & Spend)

NEW IN VERSION 32.8
- Post office deposits have their own page, like the RD page: scheme
  (Time Deposit, NSC, KVP, MIS or SCSS), term, account holder, single or
  joint, account or certificate number, post office, date and amount.
- Each scheme's rules are checked: TD, NSC and KVP from Rs 1,000 in Rs 100
  steps; MIS and SCSS in Rs 1,000 steps, up to Rs 9 lakh (Rs 15 lakh
  joint) for MIS and Rs 30 lakh for SCSS. The rules are shown on the page.
- The government rate for the date of deposit and the maturity date fill
  in by themselves. A TD shows the interest paid each year, MIS each month
  and SCSS each quarter (1 April, July, October, January), with the next
  payment; NSC shows the interest added each year and which years count
  for 80C.
- Interest paid into an account shows on Home on the day, with Add to put
  it in that account. Interest received and "Matured or closed" record the
  money coming back.
- Post office deposits are listed under Wealth > Schemes with your RD, SSY
  and PPF; Investments links to them.
- RD follows the post office rules: monthly only, from Rs 100 in Rs 10
  steps, due by the 15th (opened on the 1st to 15th) or the last working
  day. Missed instalments and the default fee are shown and filled in
  when you deposit.
- SSY and PPF deposits are checked for Rs 50 steps and the Rs 1.5 lakh
  yearly limit; SSY takes the girl's date of birth.
- The scheme calculator shows a TD's yearly interest correctly and adds
  each scheme to the right page; the savings account opens as an account.

NEW IN VERSION 32.7
- Fixed deposits work out what they pay. Under "Kind of deposit" choose a
  bank FD (interest added, or paid out) or a post office scheme: Time
  Deposit (1, 2, 3 or 5 years), NSC, Kisan Vikas Patra, Monthly Income
  Scheme or Senior Citizens Savings Scheme.
- For post office schemes the app fills in the government's rate for the
  date the deposit was opened (from April 2020) and the maturity date.
- A deposit named as a post office one ("Post office ...", "NSC", "KVP")
  is worked out from its name, even before you open it.
- A Time Deposit shows the interest it pays each year and the money that
  comes back at the end; NSC and bank FDs show the maturity amount.
- If your certificate shows a maturity amount, type it and the app uses it.
- A deposit's value grows with the interest earned so far, so net worth
  counts it.

NEW IN VERSION 32.6
- SBI statements (SSY and others) read correctly: newest-first rows with a
  dash in the empty Debit/Credit column, and balances followed forward in
  time, so deposits are no longer read as withdrawals.
- "Statement from ... to ..." and the "Brought ... Closing Balance"
  summary are read: a statement that starts on 1 April gives the bank's
  balance right after its 31 March interest.
- The money brought into a statement before the first 31 March is the
  opening deposit; Year by year works out the bank's interest for the
  year that ends at that balance.
- Importing again repairs an earlier wrong import: deposits saved as
  withdrawals are put right and a wrong starting balance is replaced.
- Scheme cards show what is in the bank now beside the value, which also
  counts the interest building up this year.

NEW IN VERSION 32.5

HOW UPDATES WORK NOW
- Changes are made in this GitHub repo. GitHub checks the app, builds the APK and
  publishes it with a version.json note of what's new.
- When you open the app and a newer build exists, an "Update available" prompt appears
  in front. Tap Update now (Android asks you to confirm the install). Tick
  "Next time, update without asking" to skip the prompt from then on.
- The source code is in the source/ folder (build with: python3 build.py).
